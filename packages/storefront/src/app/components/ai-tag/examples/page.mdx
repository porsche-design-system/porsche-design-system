## Within Checkbox

The `p-ai-tag` can be used inline with other form components. In this example, it is placed within the slotted label of
a `p-checkbox` to indicate AI-generated or AI-related content for individual options.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithCheckbox} />

## Within Radio Group

The `p-ai-tag` can also be placed within the slotted label of a `p-radio-button-wrapper` to indicate AI-related content
for individual radio options.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithRadioButton} />

## Within Form Label

The `p-ai-tag` can be placed within a `label` of a `p-select` (or any other form component) to indicate AI-related
content for selected options.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithSelect} />

## Within Input Search

The `p-ai-tag` can be placed in the `end` slot of a `p-input-search` to indicate that the search results are AI-powered.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithInputSearch} />

## Within Text

The `p-ai-tag` can be placed inline within a `p-text` to indicate that the surrounding text content is AI-related.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithText} />

## Within Image

The `p-ai-tag` can be positioned as an overlay on images to indicate that the image content has been generated or
modified by AI.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithImage} />

## Within Table

The `p-ai-tag` can sit inline in a `p-table-head-cell` next to the column title — for example on a **Status** column
when that data is AI-assisted.

<ComponentStory story={aiTagStoryWithTable} />
