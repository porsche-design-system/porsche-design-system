| <Code variant="nowrap">durationSm</Code> | Holds a **short** motion/transition duration value.     | <Code variant="value">{durationSm}</Code> |
| <Code variant="nowrap">durationMd</Code> | Holds a **moderate** motion/transition duration value.  | <Code variant="value">{durationMd}</Code> |
