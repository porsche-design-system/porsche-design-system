# Personalized Mailing

<TableOfContents headings={['Video', 'No Video']} />

A personal follow-up page for a configured vehicle, linked from a mailing. It recaps the configuration, recommends
further options and hands over to a personal sales advisor. The two variants differ in how the page opens; everything
below the opening is the same.

## Video

Opens on a full-bleed video of the vehicle, which insets into a rounded card while it scrolls away, and greets the
customer by name.

<ExampleViewer example="templates/personalized-mailing/video" />

## No Video

Opens on a giant title the configured vehicle is pulled up into – for a lighter page, or when no footage is available.
