# Popover

<TableOfContents headings={['Local market switch', 'Priority navigation', 'Feature tour', 'Responsive disclosure']} />

The popover displays contextual information or actions in a floating layer anchored to a trigger element. It helps
surface secondary content on demand without navigating away from the current context, keeping the primary interface
clean while remaining accessible.

## Local market switch

On entering the site, a popover anchored to the globe icon in the header suggests switching to the user's local market
for correct content and pricing. It is shown on load and can be dismissed via an outside click or the Escape key.

## Priority navigation

When horizontal space is limited, navigation items that no longer fit are collapsed into a popover behind a "More"
trigger, keeping the bar on a single line. Resize the frame to see items move in and out of the popover.

## Feature tour

On first visit, a sequence of popovers guides the user through the key areas of the interface one step at a time ("Step
2 of 4", Back / Next / Skip), each anchored to the element it describes. Only a single step is open at once.

## Responsive disclosure

The account menu opens as a popover on desktop, but below the `sm` breakpoint (760px) the same trigger opens a bottom
sheet instead — easier to reach on touch devices. Drag the frame narrower than 760px and open the account icon to see it
switch.
