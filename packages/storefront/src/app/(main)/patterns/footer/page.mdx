# Footer

<TableOfContents headings={['Extended', 'Simple']} />

The footer closes every page with the legal information it has to carry: consumption and emission disclaimers, the
wordmark, the copyright and the legal links. How much it offers beyond that depends on the page it belongs to.

## Extended

The footer of a site. Besides the legal information it offers the delivery country, the site navigation in columns,
social media and payment service providers, so users can reach key information from wherever they are.

<ExampleViewer example="patterns/footer/extended" />

## Simple

Nothing but the legal information. It suits a page that stands on its own, such as a campaign or a personalized landing
page, where the site navigation would lead away from what the page is about.

<ExampleViewer example="patterns/footer/simple" />
