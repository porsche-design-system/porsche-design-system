A feature highlight presents a feature of a vehicle with a mood image and reveals further details on demand, e.g. within
a model page of porsche.com. The image covers the whole `background` slot. Heading and accordion are wrapped in a single
element in the `bottom` slot. `gradient="bottom"` ensures the legibility of the heading on the image, and the
`scheme-dark` class renders a dark gradient with light content independent of the color scheme of the page.

The details are shown in a `p-accordion`, whose summary acts as trigger. `align-marker="start"` places the marker in
front of the summary and `compact` reduces its spacing. `self-stretch` lets the expanded details span the full width of
the Tile. When expanded, the Tile grows beyond its aspect ratio, and the gradient grows with the `bottom` slot. The
accordion is controlled via its `open` property and the `update` event. The Tile has no `anchor`, so the accordion is
clickable without opting in via `pointer-events-auto`.
