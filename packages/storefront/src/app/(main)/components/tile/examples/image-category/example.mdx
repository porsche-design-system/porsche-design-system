An image category tile links to a category with a mood image, e.g. a model range on porsche.com. The image covers the
whole `background` slot. The heading and an icon-only link are placed in the `bottom` slot, which centers them
vertically, and pushed apart via `ms-auto` on the link. `gradient="bottom"` ensures the legibility of the heading on the
image. The `scheme-dark` class renders a dark gradient with light content independent of the color scheme of the page,
which suits most mood images.

`cover` on the arrow link stretches its clickable area over the Tile, so the whole Tile links to the category, and
renders the focus indicator around the Tile. The label of the link is its accessible name. Since hovering the Tile
hovers the link, the link shows its own hover state, and the image zooms in via `hover-effect="zoom"`. The Tile scales
the content of the `background` slot, animates the zoom and omits it if the user prefers reduced motion and clips the
image to its corner radius.
